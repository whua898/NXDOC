Support for PMI recognition

Feature-based machining supports the following for Product and Manufacturing Information (PMI):

  • Upper and lower limits of parameters, such as depth, width, and length, for a machining feature.

    • The PMI must be defined on edges or faces associated with the feature.

    • The PMI for a depth parameter must be defined in the axial direction, and start from the top of the feature.

    • NX ignores PMI defined on chamfer depths.

    • PMI chains are not supported.

  • Upper and lower limits of the linear dimensions for a milling feature.

    • The PMI must be defined on faces associated with the feature.

    • The PMI definition must match the feature parameter.

    • PMI chains are not supported.
