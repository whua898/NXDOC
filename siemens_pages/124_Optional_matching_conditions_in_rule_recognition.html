Optional matching conditions in rule recognition

When you activate the following recognition rule options, NX uses extra matching conditions to limit the features that are recognized:

  • Names of edges or faces

  • Attributes of edges or faces

  • Colors of faces

  • Logical conditions for PMI dimensions
