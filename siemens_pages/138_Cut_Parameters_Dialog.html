Cut Direction

The Cut Direction drop down menu gives you two options: Climb Cut and Conventional Cut

Climb Cut and Conventional Cut

For information on climb and conventional cut go to: Manufacturing Milling-→ Planar and Cavity Milling-→ Machine Parameters-→ Cutting-→ Direction.

Intol/Outol

Intol and outol are the in tolerance and the out tolerance used to machine a part. Enter the in tolerance for the machining of the hole in the Intol field, and the out tolerance for the machining of the hole in the Outol field.

Part Stock (KF)

This is the stock added to the diameter of the hole, either implied by the geometry or specified by the parameter.

Part stock is the amount of material remaining on the walls of the hole after it has been machined.

Floor Stock (KF)

This is the stock added to the depth of the hole. Specify the amount of material to remain on the floor of the hole after it has been machined in the Floor Stock option.

Blank Stock

This is the stock added to the predrill hole diameter.

Blank stock is the distance the tool is positioned from the defined blank geometry. It applies to blank boundaries or blank geometry that have a tanto condition.
