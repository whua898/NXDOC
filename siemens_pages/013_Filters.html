Filters

Filters in the Machining Feature Navigator and Operation Navigator let you control what appears in the navigator.

In the Machining Feature Navigator, you can use filters to show features that:

  • Belong to a certain feature type, such as SIMPLE_HOLE.

  • Have a common attribute, such as a status of Generated.

  • Are parallel to a specified direction.

  • Are parallel to the tool axis of a specified MCS.

In the Operation Navigator, you can use filters to limit the operations shown to Hole Making operations.

Note:

Using a filter does not remove features or operations from the NC program.
