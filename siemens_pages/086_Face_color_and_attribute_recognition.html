Face color and attribute recognition

Use color and attribute recognition to help specify regions for machining that may be difficult to recognize by topology or shape. Face attributes may also include a value that could determine or influence the recognition result.

You can specify any combination of face colors and face attributes for feature recognition of solid body or sheet body regions.
